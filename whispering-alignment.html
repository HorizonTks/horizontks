AI Whispering & Alignment

AI systems should not just function. They should represent you.

What it is

Careful refinement of agent instructions, communication style, operating boundaries, and practical behaviors so AI remains useful and consistent with the people and organization it serves.

Who it is for

Teams already using AI agents that want greater consistency, better judgment within defined limits, and a thoughtful process for ongoing improvement.

What you walk away with

Refined agent instructions and operating boundaries, representative test scenarios, documented behavior improvements, and recommendations for future review. Deeper engagements can address organizational voice and consistency across multiple agents. Ongoing arrangements can include periodic alignment checks and adjustments.

What it costs to start

Focused alignment engagements typically range from $750 to $2,500+, depending on scope and complexity. Deeper agent-behavior and organizational voice projects, and ongoing alignment support, are quoted separately.

Retainers are optional and defined in writing; no open-ended commitment is assumed.
