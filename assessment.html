AI Operating Environment Assessment

Before investing in more AI, understand the environment you already have.

What it is

A structured review of your current AI use, including how people interact with tools and agents, where information moves, who makes decisions, and what is or is not working.

Who it is for

Small organizations and teams using AI in real work, especially those moving beyond isolated experiments and looking for greater clarity.

What you walk away with

A written summary of the current environment, a prioritized list of risks and opportunities, and practical next steps. Recommendations are useful on their own; there is no obligation to commission further work.

What it costs to start

Scope and information requirements are agreed before payment. No system credentials or confidential records are needed for an initial inquiry.
