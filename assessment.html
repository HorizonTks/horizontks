AI Operating Environment Assessment

Before investing in more AI, understand the environment you already have.

What it is

A structured review of your current AI use, including how people interact with tools and agents, where information moves, who makes decisions, and what is or is not working. We examine whether anticipated productivity gains are supported by evidence, which work should remain under human judgment, and where operational capacity could be put at risk.

Who it is for

Small organizations and teams using AI in real work, especially those moving beyond isolated experiments and looking for greater clarity.

What you walk away with

A documented current-state assessment informed by agreed stakeholder discovery, a prioritized analysis of risks and opportunities, and practical recommendations for what to address first. Where the agreed scope permits, findings distinguish verified capabilities from assumptions and identify validation steps before consequential changes. Recommendations are useful on their own; there is no obligation to commission further work.

What it costs to start

Professional assessments typically range from $2,500 to $5,000, depending on organizational scope, stakeholder involvement, and the depth of review. Deliverables and price are agreed in writing before work begins.
