Reclaiming AI

The question is not simply what AI can do. It is what kind of human experience we are building around it.

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of how we work, think, create, and make decisions. Its value cannot be measured by speed alone. The systems we build shape the conditions in which people collaborate, exercise judgment, and discover what matters. Reclaiming AI is an invitation to design those conditions with care.
