Who We Are

Designing Human + AI Operating Environments

Our Mission

Horizon Technologys helps organizations design coherent operating environments in which people, information, process, technology, and increasingly capable AI systems work together with clear purpose.

Our work begins with a simple premise: adding AI does not eliminate the need for systems architecture. It makes architecture more important. Organizations still need to decide what humans should decide, what AI should handle, where authoritative information should live, how specialized agents should coordinate, and when human judgment must take over.

We help make those decisions deliberate, practical, and aligned with the work the organization is actually trying to accomplish.

What We Do

Agentic AI Systems Architecture is the design of the larger human + AI system: the roles, responsibilities, information flows, decision boundaries, handoffs, controls, and feedback loops that allow people and AI agents to work together effectively.

The objective is not to put AI everywhere. It is to put the right intelligence in the right places... with the right information, authority, coordination, and human oversight.

Human + AI Operating Models

Define how people and AI agents divide work, exercise judgment, collaborate, escalate, and remain accountable.

Workflow, Roles & Handoffs

Design processes in which specialized agents and human participants have clear responsibilities, boundaries, and transitions.

Knowledge, Information & State Architecture

Establish where authoritative information lives, how context is preserved, and how systems remain coherent across tools, agents, and decisions.

Governance, Evaluation & Quality

Build human-in-the-loop controls, evaluation methods, and quality standards that keep AI useful, reliable, and aligned with organizational purpose.

The Horizon Perspective

"The technology has changed. The architecture problem has not." — Robert E Miller, Founder & Principal Consultant

For nearly three decades, Horizon Technologys has worked at the intersection of people, information, technology, workflow, and organizational change. Agentic AI introduces extraordinary new capabilities, but the central systems questions remain familiar: What are we trying to accomplish? Who or what should do the work? What information is authoritative? Where does judgment belong? How do the parts coordinate? How do we know the system is working?

That systems perspective is the foundation we bring to AI transformation today.
